Welcome

A place where every child is known, loved and inspired

Adventist education is about more than passing exams. It is the harmonious development of the mental, physical, social and spiritual powers — preparing students for joyful service in this world and for the life to come.

Our dedicated teachers partner with families to nurture curious learners, confident leaders and compassionate servants of their communities.
